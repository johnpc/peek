# Privacy Policy — peek

**peek – Screen Sharing** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- **No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.** The app has no backend. The only network
  traffic is the VNC/Screen Sharing connection between your device and the computer you
  choose to connect to.
- **Passwords** you choose to remember are stored only in your device's iOS Keychain and
  are sent only to the computer you are connecting to, as part of that connection.
- **Saved computers** (names and addresses) and your preferences stay on your device.
- **Local network access and Bonjour** are used solely to find Macs that advertise Screen
  Sharing on your network. Nothing discovered is sent anywhere.
- **Clipboard** contents are shared only between your device and the remote computer, and
  only when you use Paste or the remote computer copies text.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to delete or export. Questions:
